About us

Unyielding Protection, Uncompromising Comfort

Founded in 2016, STRIKERS was born from the belief that extreme weather shouldn't mean compromising on style. We engineer technical apparel that performs in the harshest conditions while maintaining a clean, minimal aesthetic.

Our materials are sourced from Japan and Italy, combining cutting-edge fabric technology with timeless design. Every piece is built to last — because we believe true sustainability means creating clothes you'll never want to replace.
